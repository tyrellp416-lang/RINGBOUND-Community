# 📅 When Can I Step Into the Ring?

### RINGBOUND's current testing and release outlook
**Updated October 18, 2026**

We know the biggest question: **when can you actually play RINGBOUND?**

We're building an Android-first, text-driven wrestling game that grows into a persistent online universe. Match simulation is already being developed and tested internally. The wider experience, including careers, promotions, championships, shared history and connected players, still needs substantial work.

Below are our **best current planning windows**, not booked release dates or promises. We'll move a window when the experience or testing evidence calls for it rather than release a broken build to meet a calendar.

| Stage | Current planning window | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🥊 **Invite-only private alpha** | **July–September 2027 (Q3)** | A small group helping us test the match experience: preparation, strategy, moves, outcomes, clarity and Android usability. This may be a limited, match-focused experience rather than a complete online career. |
| 👑 **Expanded private world testing** | **January–June 2028 (Q1–Q2)** | A controlled group exploring early wrestler development, career decisions, bookings, promotions, records and persistence as those systems become ready. |
| 🌍 **Public testing** | **July–December 2028 (Q3–Q4)** | Broader access to a meaningful connected RINGBOUND experience, with feedback, stability work and real-world balancing. The precise scope and access method remain undecided. |
| 🏆 **RINGBOUND 1.0: Main Event** | **October–December 2029 (Q4 target)** | Our current target for a stable, player-ready living wrestling universe. **Delays into 2030 remain possible.** |

## Why the longer runway?

A convincing wrestling match is only the first bell. We also need a wrestler you can grow, a career that remembers your choices, promotions and championships that carry history, secure online accounts and fair shared competition, and an Android experience that works reliably.

We're investing in believable match decisions and results first because every future career and championship depends on that foundation. The private alpha can start earlier with a deliberately narrower scope; public testing needs more of the world to be functional.

## What has to be ready?

**Before outside private alpha:** a stable and comprehensible match loop; fair outcomes and reproducible match results; sufficient move and finishing variety to provide meaningful feedback; Android installation, usability and crash checks; and a safe way to collect tester reports.

**Before wider private world tests:** repeatable career and progression loops, trustworthy saved records, useful booking/promotion features, account/data boundaries and recovery tests appropriate to the invited scope.

**Before public testing:** an enjoyable returning-player loop; secure online state and legitimate match results; basic support and moderation; sensible performance; save/data protections; and successful controlled testing.

**Before 1.0:** the core promise of a connected wrestling world must hold together. That includes reliable careers, meaningful promotions/championships/history, fair competition, accessible onboarding, stable Android delivery, security, backups and load-tested operations.

A stage opens only after its readiness checks pass. Dates can move **earlier or later** as real results emerge.
