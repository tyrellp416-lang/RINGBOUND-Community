# 📅 When Can I Step Into the Ring?

### Your road to the RINGBOUND universe
**Updated October 19, 2026**

**Every Match Matters. Every Decision Echoes. Every Legacy Is Earned.**

The question we've been hearing is simple: **when can I play RINGBOUND?**

We're building an Android-first, text-driven wrestling experience with a bigger ambition: a persistent online world where you create a wrestler, build a career, forge rivalries, chase championships and leave a legacy that other players can remember.

The match engine is already in internal development and testing. Careers, promotions, shared history and the wider online world are still being built. Here's how we currently see the road to the first bell.

**These are planning windows, not confirmed launch dates.** Each stage depends on the game being ready for the people invited to play. We'll adjust the schedule when testing tells us we should.

## 🥊 The Road to the Ring

### 2027 | The First Bell
**Invite-only private alpha: July–September 2027 (Q3)**

The first outside testers step between the ropes. This early experience is expected to focus on the heart of RINGBOUND: making match decisions, choosing a strategy, watching moves unfold and discovering how a contest can turn on a single moment.

It may be a focused match-testing build rather than a complete online career. The goal is to make every exchange feel meaningful before we expand the world around it.

### 2028 | A World Begins to Take Shape
**Expanded private world testing: January–June 2028 (Q1–Q2)**

Wrestling becomes bigger than one match. As development allows, a controlled group will explore early careers, wrestler growth, bookings, promotions and records that remember what happened.

This is where we begin testing whether your choices can carry weight beyond the final bell.

### 2028 | Open the Doors
**Public testing: July–December 2028 (Q3–Q4)**

Our current window for bringing more players into a connected RINGBOUND experience. We'll be listening closely, improving stability, balancing competition and learning what makes players want to return for the next show.

The exact features, number of available places and access method will be announced when they're ready.

### 2029 | The Main Event
**RINGBOUND 1.0 target: October–December 2029 (Q4)**

The long-term goal is a living wrestling universe where careers evolve, promotions build their own histories, and victories and defeats become part of something bigger.

**Late 2029 is our current target, with a move into 2030 still possible.** A lasting wrestling world deserves a strong foundation.

## 🔔 Why Does It Take Time?

Because the dream goes beyond simulating a match.

We want the wrestler you create to have a journey worth following. We want rivalries to develop, championships to mean something and decisions to shape what happens next. That takes believable matches, dependable career systems, secure online play and a world that remembers its own history.

We're starting with the action inside the ring. Everything that follows depends on getting that right.

## 🛠️ What Are We Working Toward?

Each testing stage has its own readiness checks.

- **First private alpha:** Matches that are understandable, varied and fair, with reliable Android performance and a way for testers to report issues.
- **Expanded private testing:** Early career and booking systems that work together, along with dependable records and saved progress.
- **Public testing:** A connected experience worth returning to, supported by stable online systems, player protections and successful earlier tests.
- **Version 1.0:** A dependable foundation for careers, promotions, championships, lasting history and fair competition.

We'll open each stage when the experience meets its readiness checks. **The windows may move earlier or later as development progresses.**

## 🎟️ How Can I Be Part of It?

**Playtesting applications are currently closed.** There are no active invitations, public builds, waitlists or registration forms.

When applications open, we'll share the details through our community channels. Following RINGBOUND keeps you close to development news, although it doesn't guarantee a testing invitation.
