# 📅 When Can I Step Into the Ring?

### The RINGBOUND development outlook
**Updated November 11, 2026 · Conditional AI-first scenarios**

**Every Match Matters. Every Decision Echoes. Every Legacy Is Earned.**

RINGBOUND is an Android-first, text-driven wrestling simulation in development. The long-term ambition is a persistent online world with careers, rivalries, promotions, championships and lasting history.

## 🥊 How we're actually building RINGBOUND

**Claude Code and Codex are our two AI developers.** The project owner directs the experience, makes gameplay and architectural decisions, authorizes changes, coordinates handoffs and verifies important Android builds.

That lets us create and test engineering candidates quickly, but a feature is only ready when it has passed its own rigorous qualification. The last **officially promoted game baseline is still 0.2.9 Crimson Canvas / Build 36**. The next milestone, **0.2.10 Beyond the Ropes**, remains in development; independent match-engine architecture research is also underway.

Our November evidence review found substantial code, test and diagnostic progress since October 2, but **no newly promoted gameplay milestone** in that measured period. For that reason, the outlook below shows **possible planning scenarios**, not booked release dates or confirmed invitations.

**These ranges are exploratory scenarios with substantial uncertainty.** The accelerated column assumes the agents can repeatedly deliver *fully qualified* milestones, integrate them cleanly, and secure the connected world. We have not proved that rate yet. The working column is for planning only and may shift in either direction.

The previous October 29 planning estimate targeted invite-only matches in Q4 2028–Q2 2029, private career testing in 2030, connected testing in 2031, and 1.0 in H2 2032, with risk extending beyond 2033. **That earlier estimate is preserved as a historical baseline.** H2 2032 falls inside today's broad working scenario.

## 🔍 What determines these dates?

**First private match alpha:** legal and understandable wrestling outcomes, credible damage and match pacing, consistent text presentation, usable Android builds, and tester feedback tools. An early alpha can be *match-only*, without a full online MMO.

**Expanded career/world test:** durable wrestler and match records, booking and persistent consequences that actually work together. Live human-versus-human PvP remains a separate future qualification.

**Public connected test:** secure accounts, trustworthy server-authoritative results, abuse/moderation protections, persistence, monitoring and support, with meaningful prior testing.

**1.0:** a reliable connected world with sustainable careers, promotion history, meaningful competition, accessibility and operational readiness.

We will revisit these scenarios after the current match-engine architectural review and when multiple further gameplay milestones actually pass qualification. Research, an impressive test count and a working prototype can all be meaningful progress without representing a public-ready release.
