03 / QUESTIONS FROM RINGSIDE

Before you put your name on the card.

Can I apply to test RINGBOUND today?

Applications are closed. We're building and testing the game first. Join our Discord for official recruitment news.

Will joining Discord reserve my place?

No. Discord is where you can follow news and connect with the community. Future applications and invitations will have their own process.

Do I need to be a wrestling expert?

Not necessarily. We hope to hear from many kinds of players, including people with experience in wrestling games and complete newcomers.

When does private or public testing begin?

No date has been announced. A first test will happen when a build is useful enough to give players a meaningful experience and clear feedback goals.
