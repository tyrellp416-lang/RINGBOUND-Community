## Enter the ring

Create a wrestler. Build a reputation. Find allies, make rivals, chase championships, and leave a mark on a world that remembers what happened.

**RINGBOUND** is an in-development online wrestling universe built around lasting careers, meaningful matches, and stories that grow from players' decisions. Every contest is part of a bigger history.

## A world with a memory

- **Your wrestler, your legacy.** Grow your abilities, earn opportunities, and develop an identity over time.
- **Matches with consequences.** The road to victory is shaped by preparation, wrestling decisions, momentum, and the unexpected.
- **Rivalries worth remembering.** Alliances, betrayals, wins, losses, and title reigns become part of the world's history.
- **Promotions and championships.** Work your way through the wrestling world, with a long-term vision for player-run promotions and connected competition.
- **Wrestling told through words.** A text-first experience designed to make the action, tactics, and drama come alive.

## Development ringside

RINGBOUND is **in active development**. The current focus is building a deeper, more believable wrestling simulation and testing how moves, decisions, positioning, officiating, and match endings work together.

| Area | Public status |
| --- | --- |
| Core match simulation | Active development and testing |
| Move identity and finishing behavior | Internal research and testing |
| Outside-the-ring action | Internal development and testing |
| Wrestler careers and progression | Design and foundation work |
| Promotions, rivalries, and living history | Long-term development direction |

For the latest curated update, see the [development roadmap](ROADMAP.md), [development log](DEVLOG.md), or [status data](status.json). Feature plans may change as testing continues.
