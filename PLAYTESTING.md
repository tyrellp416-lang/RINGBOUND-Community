# 🎟️ Interested in Testing RINGBOUND?

### Help shape a wrestling world from the beginning.

Maybe you're the kind of fan who notices when a pinfall feels wrong. Maybe you want to see if a technical wrestler can outsmart a powerhouse. Maybe you just want to create a character and see where the story takes you.

**Those perspectives could all be valuable when RINGBOUND is ready for outside testing.**

## Explore the official tester portal

The [**RINGBOUND Tester Portal**](https://ringbound-testers.vercel.app) is now live. You can explore the future application experience, but **submissions remain closed** and no personal details are being collected. Join our Discord for recruitment news.

## Where are we now?

**RINGBOUND is in active development.**

There is **no playable public test and no open tester application** right now. We want to share a build when there is a meaningful experience worth evaluating.

Our current **non-binding forecasts** are:

- **Private invite-only match alpha:** October 2028–June 2029 (Q4 2028–Q2 2029).
- **Expanded private career/world testing:** January–September 2030 (Q1–Q3), without a promise of human PvP.
- **Public connected testing:** April–December 2031 (Q2–Q4), if secure online and support gates qualify.
- **RINGBOUND 1.0 working target:** July–December 2032 (H2), with delays into 2033 or later possible.

[📅 Read the full timeline and testing readiness conditions](TIMELINE.md). These windows are estimates, not dates when signups will open.

## How to hear about future opportunities

1. **[Join our Discord](https://discord.gg/RkmATFsk)** for community news and announcements.
2. **[Follow r/ringboundmmo](https://www.reddit.com/r/ringboundmmo/)** for discussion and project updates.
3. **[Keep an eye on the Ringside Report](DEVLOG.md)** as development progresses.

When a test is formally announced, we'll explain how to participate, what devices are supported, and what feedback we're looking for. Joining the community does not guarantee a playtest invitation.

## What makes a great tester?

You don't need to be a programmer. Some of the most useful observations are simple:

- "That comeback felt incredible."
- "I couldn't tell why my wrestler lost."
- "The same move happened too many times."
- "The match told a story I wanted to share."
- "This part confused me."

We want the finished experience to be understandable, believable, and worth coming back to.
