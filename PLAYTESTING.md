# 🎟️ Interested in Testing RINGBOUND?

### Help shape a wrestling world from the beginning.

Maybe you're the kind of fan who notices when a pinfall feels wrong. Maybe you want to see if a technical wrestler can outsmart a powerhouse. Maybe you just want to create a character and see where the story takes you.

**Those perspectives could all be valuable when RINGBOUND is ready for outside testing.**

## Where are we now?

**RINGBOUND is in active development.**

There is **no public playtest available, no announced test date, and no open tester application** right now. We want to share a build when there is a meaningful experience worth evaluating.

## How to hear about future opportunities

1. **[Join our Discord](https://discord.gg/RkmATFsk)** for community news and announcements.
2. **[Follow r/ringboundmmo](https://www.reddit.com/r/ringboundmmo/)** for discussion and project updates.
3. **[Keep an eye on the Ringside Report](DEVLOG.md)** as development progresses.

If and when a public test is announced, we'll explain how to participate, what devices are supported, and what feedback we're looking for. Joining the community does not guarantee a playtest invitation.

## What makes a great tester?

You don't need to be a programmer. Some of the most useful observations are simple:

- "That comeback felt incredible."
- "I couldn't tell why my wrestler lost."
- "The same move happened too many times."
- "The match told a story I wanted to share."
- "This part confused me."

We want the finished experience to be understandable, believable, and worth coming back to.
