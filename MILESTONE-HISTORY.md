# 🥊 RINGBOUND: The Story So Far

**Every Match Matters. Every Decision Echoes. Every Legacy Is Earned.**

Before RINGBOUND becomes a world where wrestlers build careers and write history,
we have to make the matches themselves worth remembering.

These are **completed internal development milestones** that helped build
RINGBOUND's early match foundation. They are milestones in the making of the
game, **not public releases, playable downloads, or testing announcements**.

## September 2026: Smarter Choices in the Ring

### 0.2.7 | Fight IQ

A believable wrestler needs more than a collection of moves. They need to
recognize when to push forward, protect a lead, change tactics, or go for a
finish.

Fight IQ laid groundwork for understanding the choices behind a match. We
started building ways to judge the situation and compare different approaches,
while carefully checking that no single approach could dominate every match.

**Why players should care:** A great victory should feel earned. Better
decision-making foundations help us build matches where a wrestler's choices
and circumstances tell a story.

*Recorded in the September 2026 internal development milestone series.*

## September 2026: Matches with Their Own Personality

### 0.2.8 | Match DNA

Two matches shouldn't feel identical just because they follow the same rules.
We developed better ways to see a contest's rhythm, recognize different
styles, and understand what makes one matchup different from another.

Some more experimental style systems remained switched off while we evaluated
their effects. This milestone strengthened our understanding of matches rather
than promising every idea was ready for players.

**Why players should care:** Different wrestlers and matchups should create
different kinds of stories, without one style automatically becoming the best.

*Internal milestone recorded September 30, 2026.*

## October 2026: When a Match Leaves Its Mark

### 0.2.9 | Crimson Canvas

A bruising contest should leave a mark on the story being told. This milestone
brought together clearer written match presentation and better tracking of
physical consequences, including wounds and the referee's part in stopping a
match when necessary.

**Why players should care:** If a wrestler takes a beating, fights through
danger, or can no longer safely continue, those moments should have a reason
and a consequence. The story should help you understand what happened.

*Internal milestone qualified October 2, 2026.*

## What's being built next?

environment. It will receive a completed-milestone update only after the
remaining tests, reviews, and public announcement approval are finished.

**The milestones above describe progress in internal development.**

closed, and target dates may change as the game develops.
