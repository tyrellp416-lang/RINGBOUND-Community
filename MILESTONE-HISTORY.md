# 🥊 RINGBOUND: The Story So Far

**Every Match Matters. Every Decision Echoes. Every Legacy Is Earned.**

These are completed **internal development milestones**, not public playable releases or open testing invitations.

## 0.2.7 | Fight IQ (September 29, 2026)

**What we worked on:** We developed smarter match-decision foundations, including how wrestlers consider offense, defense, and opportunities to finish a contest. We refined safeguards against runaway momentum and worked on making finishers more decisive. This milestone did not activate every planned strategic behavior.

**Why it matters to players:** A wrestler protecting a lead should not think like a wrestler who needs a comeback. Matches should develop through ability and choices rather than endless one-sided loops.

## 0.2.8 | Match DNA (September 30, 2026)

**What we worked on:** We built match-analysis and presentation foundations to recognize pace, memorable moments, and differences between contests. Experimental lane-affinity and adaptation systems remained **OFF** while their balance was evaluated.

**Why it matters to players:** Technical contests and aggressive brawls should eventually tell recognizably different stories. These foundations help us build that variety without pretending experimental systems were released.

## 0.2.9 | Crimson Canvas (October 2, 2026)

**What we worked on:** We refined written match presentation and connected wound information to the action being described. Internal testing also covered referee-stoppage handling.

**Why it matters to players:** If a wrestler accumulates damage, the commentary should acknowledge it. When a referee intervenes, the moment should fit the danger unfolding in the ring.
