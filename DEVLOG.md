### Notes from the road to RINGBOUND

Every great wrestling world starts somewhere. This is where we share the moments that bring ours closer to life.

## October 29, 2026
### 📅 A revised outlook, grounded in the work still ahead

We've refreshed our estimates after reviewing the actual match-development milestone, remaining wrestler and combat depth, career systems, promotions and secure online-world requirements.

Our revised **provisional windows** are: small invite-only match alpha **Q4 2028–Q2 2029**, expanded private career/world testing **Q1–Q3 2030**, public connected testing **Q2–Q4 2031**, and a working RINGBOUND 1.0 release target in **H2 2032**. A move into 2033 or later remains possible.

The qualified internal match foundation is real, but later features are not yet released. We want to make wrestling feel convincing, then make careers and the world reliably remember what happened, before opening secure online competition.

**These dates are planning forecasts, not an announcement of playable builds or applications. Applications remain closed.** The dated October 18 forecast below is preserved as an earlier, now-superseded estimate.

## October 18, 2026
### 📅 Sharing our first long-range release outlook

We've taken another look at how much RINGBOUND still needs before players can experience the full living universe. We want future testers to know what we're aiming for, without promising dates that the game hasn't earned.

Our current **provisional targets** are a small **private match alpha in Q3 2027**, expanded private career/world tests in **Q1–Q2 2028**, **public testing in Q3–Q4 2028**, and **RINGBOUND 1.0 in Q4 2029**, with a possible slip into 2030.

These windows are **estimates**, subject to gameplay quality, security and reliability gates. **Applications are closed.** We'll share changes openly as evidence arrives.

## October 18, 2026
### 🥊 Building the moments that make a match

**The focus: more believable wrestling, more meaningful decisions.**

Think about the moments you remember from a great match. The sudden counter when defeat seemed certain. The big move that changes everything. The exhaustion that turns a routine escape into a desperate struggle.

Those are the kinds of moments we're working to bring to RINGBOUND.

Right now, our internal development and testing is exploring how wrestlers:

- Choose moves that suit the moment and their abilities
- Respond to fatigue, danger, position, and momentum
- Build toward convincing finishing opportunities
- Use the ring and ringside space
- Interact with referees, rules, and match endings

### Why that matters

A match becomes memorable when you can understand **why it unfolded the way it did**. We want victories to feel earned, upsets to feel possible, and the story of a bout to be worth talking about afterward.

**Where things stand:** This is ongoing internal work. We're still testing and refining it. Our published timeline offers provisional windows, but no public build or confirmed test date is available yet.
