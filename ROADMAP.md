## 🥊 Chapter One: Make the match matter

**Where we're concentrating our work now**

Before a wrestler can become a legend, stepping into the ring has to mean something.

| What we're working on | Why you'll care |
| --- | --- |
| **Matches that have a rhythm** | Wrestlers build momentum, tire out, recover, and search for the right opening. |
| **Moves with personality** | A suplex, a counter, and a finishing move should feel different in the story of a match. |
| **A real fight for the finish** | A desperate pin, a smart submission, or a count-out should have a believable reason behind it. |
| **More than four corners** | Ring ropes, the apron, and the ringside floor can create danger and opportunity. |
| **A referee who matters** | Counts, rope breaks, and rules should be part of the action, not an afterthought. |
| **Real wrestling identity and strategy** | Editable wrestler creation, learned moves, chain wrestling and scouting-informed Auto Gameplan are approved future designs, not currently public gameplay. |

These areas are being developed and tested internally. **They are not a list of features available to the public today.**

## 🌟 Chapter Two: Build your wrestler

**Career features and progression are part of the wider development journey.**

Your wrestling story should take more than a handful of victories to tell.

- **Earn your place.** Start small and work toward bigger opportunities.
- **Develop a style.** Learn moves, strengthen your abilities, and build an identity.
- **Prepare for the moment.** Make choices before a match that influence how you approach it.
- **Face the consequences.** Injuries, setbacks, reputation, and recovery can change the road ahead.
- **Find your path.** Become a feared specialist, a crowd favorite, an unlikely survivor, or something nobody expected.

We want winning to feel earned and growth to feel personal.

## 🌍 Chapter Three: Let the world remember

**A longer-term vision for a connected wrestling universe.**

Picture a world where a title isn't just a symbol. It has a history. A promotion has its own identity. Your biggest enemy has reasons to dislike you. And a rookie can become the person everyone else is chasing.

We're designing toward:

- **Promotions and championships** with meaningful places in the world
- **Alliances, rivalries, and wrestling friendships** that develop over time
- **Player-driven stories** alongside an evolving wrestling landscape
- **Historic title reigns and memorable careers** that live beyond a single match
- **Future player-owned promotions** as part of the long-term direction
