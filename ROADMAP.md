# RINGBOUND: Development Roadmap

*Updated October 18, 2026*

RINGBOUND is being built piece by piece. Our goal is a wrestling universe that feels alive, where every match adds another chapter to a wrestler's career.

This roadmap shows **areas of focus**, rather than promised release dates. Work may be refined, rescheduled, or redesigned as testing reveals what makes the game better.

## In the ring: active work

### Smarter matches

We're working toward bouts where wrestlers respond to momentum, fatigue, opportunities, and danger in convincing ways. Wins and losses should have believable causes.

### Moves that feel distinct

A wrestler's move selection, positioning, counters, and finishing attempts should matter. This work is undergoing internal research and testing.

### Action beyond the ropes

Count-outs, ringside situations, and physical surroundings are part of making matches feel complete. These systems are in internal development and testing.

## Beyond a single match: longer-term direction

### Meaningful careers

Train, improve, make choices, and build credibility over time. The vision is for careers to be shaped by talent, preparation, opportunity, and the consequences of competition.

### A connected wrestling world

Promotions, rosters, championship histories, rivalries, and player relationships should give every wrestler a place in a world that keeps evolving.

### A history worth writing

We want memorable wins, shocking upsets, long championship reigns, and legendary rivalries to remain part of RINGBOUND's shared history.

## What to expect next

Public updates will focus on **confirmed progress and player-facing improvements**, rather than raw developer activity. Internal experiments are not announcements of finished features.
