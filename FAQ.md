# ❓ RINGBOUND: Your Questions, Answered

### New around here? Here's the quick rundown.

## What is RINGBOUND?

An **upcoming text-driven online wrestling universe** where you create a wrestler, compete in meaningful matches, build a career, and help shape a world with its own ongoing history.

## Is it like a traditional wrestling video game?

RINGBOUND takes a different approach. **Wrestling is told through text, decisions, and the unfolding story of each match.** The goal is to capture the drama, strategy, and consequences of wrestling without requiring a 3D action game.

## Can I create my own wrestler?

**That's central to the vision.** Your wrestler's abilities, identity, relationships, and career decisions are intended to matter over time.

## Will there be championships, rivalries, and promotions?

**They're key parts of the planned world.** The goal is for championships to have histories, rivals to remember each other, and promotions to feel like places worth fighting for. Player-owned promotions are part of the longer-term vision.

## Do matches happen instantly?

The planned career experience puts emphasis on being booked, preparing, competing, and living with the consequences. We're also exploring quicker ways to enjoy a match. Details may change before release.

## Is there a public test I can play?

**Not yet.** No public playtest or public test date has been announced. [Follow future testing news here.](PLAYTESTING.md)

## When is RINGBOUND coming out?

**There is no announced public release date.** We're concentrating on making the underlying experience feel right before making promises.
