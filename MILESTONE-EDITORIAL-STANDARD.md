# RINGBOUND Development Updates: Editorial Standard

**Status:** Approved. Applies to every player-facing milestone update and every rewritten historical chapter.

## The reader deserves specifics

Explain what was **actually implemented**, not simply that wrestling will feel
"more realistic" or that a feature makes the game "better." Every update answers:

1. **What we built:** Identify the mechanic or system, its concrete behavior,
   and which in-match situations it affects.
2. **Why it matters:** Show an example a future player can picture, such as
   protecting a lead, surviving a comeback, or recognizing accumulating wounds.
3. **What is still unfinished:** Clearly distinguish implemented, tested
   foundations from experiments, feature-off pathways, ongoing qualification,
   future ambitions, and playable releases.
4. **What supports the claim:** Link to the already public milestone history,
   development log or roadmap. Approval in `milestones.json` must be explicit.

## Voice

Use clear, confident, human language. Be exciting because the detail is
interesting, not because the wording exaggerates it. Explain technical terms
only when they help readers understand something tangible. Avoid filler such as
"improved the experience" without saying what changed.

## Approval and publishing

Milestones are **not playable releases**. RINGBOUND is still under development
and testing applications are not open. A roadmap entry, draft, research branch,
or partial test does not automatically become a completed milestone.

The public editorial record for approved updates is
[`milestones.json`](milestones.json). Changes to existing historical
announcements require deliberate revision, not duplicate posting. The automated
Sentinel tracker must not invent, exaggerate or announce unapproved completions.
